# Product Experience — the emotional journey

The product is not a dashboard. It is a living representation of the member. This document
defines what a member should *feel* at each moment of ownership, and the design rules that
produce those feelings.

> Build the Food System Inside You. Watch it learn. See it improve. Feel the difference.

---

## The journey

### After 10 seconds
**"That's… me."**
The first screen is the body, alive — breathing aura, drifting particles, the digestive tract
glowing. A greeting and one sentence of self-status ("Your Food System is learning and getting
stronger"). No table, no grid of numbers. The single score appears *on* the stage, already
counting up, with its one-word verdict (Improving).

*Feeling: recognition + wonder.*

### After 1 minute
**"It reacts to what I do."**
They log a meal — or press Simulate in the demo — and watch the reveal: the modal closes, the
stage takes over, pathways light on the figure one by one while the same impacts appear as
sentences on the right ("Fermented foods — probiotic pathways brighten"). They scroll once and
find *what changed today* and *what the system learned*, written as sentences with reasons.

*Feeling: cause-and-effect. "My choices do something, visibly, immediately."*

### After 5 minutes
**"I understand something I didn't."**
They've hovered a hotspot and read why the gut is the centre; they've seen the after-meal journey
(eat → microbes feed → compounds made → body benefits); they've met one clear Next Best Action
with a *why this matters*. They leave knowing one true thing about their biology — without ever
feeling lectured.

*Feeling: understanding + a plan. Curiosity rewarded.*

### After 30 days
**"It knows me, and I'm different."**
The figure has evolved — rings earned, aura warmer, mood steadier. The journey rail shows named
milestones ("First meal logged · Day 1", "Fibre habit built · Day 14"). Weekly reviews ("Your
Food System This Week") read like chapters of their own story: momentum, patterns, consistency.
The daily question that brings them back is never "what's my number" — it's **"what changed
today?"**

*Feeling: ownership + momentum. The streak matters because the organism grew, not because a
counter incremented.*

### After 6 months
**"This is part of how I live — and it's bigger than me."**
The system anticipates (forecast: "a fermented breakfast would steady this afternoon's energy"),
remembers (patterns across seasons), and connects outward — the inside-out story (You → Family →
Community → … → The Food System) is now lived, not just watched. They've shared their Food System
card; maybe brought family in.

*Feeling: identity + contribution.*

## Feelings to design FOR

| Feeling | How the design produces it |
|---|---|
| **Wonder** | The living figure; choreographed reveals; dark cinematic biology sections |
| **Curiosity** | "What changed today?" chips; hotspots that answer on touch; insight of the day |
| **Hope** | Verdicts are trajectories ("Improving"), not grades; deltas over absolutes |
| **Understanding** | Every number arrives inside a sentence with a *why*; teach-by-contrast cards |
| **Ownership** | "My Food System" voice; the figure that is *theirs* (sex-matched, evolving) |
| **Progress** | Milestones with names and days; evolution rings; weekly chapters |
| **Empowerment** | One Next Best Action at a time, always food-first, always doable today |

## Feelings to design OUT

| Feeling | How we avoid it |
|---|---|
| **Guilt** | A poor meal is *strain the system handles* ("your system is buffering this — here's what helps"), never a red F. Amber, not red; recovery framing, not punishment |
| **Fear** | Non-medical language everywhere; no diagnosis words; red-flag symptoms → "talk to your GP" |
| **Confusion** | One score, one action, one insight per screen-moment; detail on demand only |
| **Data overload** | No gauge rows, no triple-repeated scores, no tables on the Overview |
| **Streak anxiety** | Missing a day dims gently and recovers fast; the organism rests, it doesn't die |

## The daily return loop

1. **Open** → greeting + "what changed today" (something is *always* different — the system
   learned, a pattern moved, a milestone approached).
2. **Act** → log a meal → the reveal (the dopamine beat is watching the body respond).
3. **Learn** → one insight, one why.
4. **Tomorrow's hook** → the Next Best Action seeds what to try; the forecast hints at what the
   system expects. Curiosity, not obligation, drives the return.

## Voice

The Food System speaks in first person about itself and second person about the member:
"I noticed…", "Your fibre diversity grew". Warm, precise, never clinical, never cheerleader-y.
Externally it is only ever **Your Food System** — "Digital Twin" stays in the engine room.
