## Feed · Seed · Rejuvenate

Action categories, not score categories. **No number is attached to any of them**, and none of them maps to a Biotic — an action is Feed because of what it is, not because of which domain surfaced it.

- **Feed** — Giving what is already there more to work with — a wider range of plant foods, and more of the fibre-rich ones.
- **Seed** — Bringing foods transformed by fermentation into the week — yoghurt, kefir, kimchi, sauerkraut, miso — regularly rather than occasionally.
- **Rejuvenate** — Renewing and protecting the pattern you already have — the rhythm of your week, and the meals that slip first when it gets busy.

## The three horizons

**A horizon says when you do it, never when it works.** No entry below carries a predicted outcome, and no frequency is a duration until an effect.

| Horizon | Answers | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** | What is one thing I could do now? | One thing, once. Small enough that a bad day does not stop it. |
| **This week** | What could I keep up across the week? | A few times across the week, attached to meals you already eat. |
| **The next 30 days** | What is worth holding long enough to find out? | One behaviour, held for a month. What survives an ordinary week is the part that has actually changed. |
