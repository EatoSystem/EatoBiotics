## The label that governs everything downstream

> ### FSS-v1 Candidate Domains — Frozen for Scientific Review, Not Yet Scientifically Approved
>
> **Diversity · Plants & Fibre · Fermented Foods · Food Quality · Meal Rhythm**

*Frozen* means no further architectural iteration. It does **not** mean
validated. The label exists so a product-architecture decision is never
mistaken for scientific approval — including by us, later, when the origin of
the list has faded.

## What is not decided here

The design spec gates Phases 3–6 on named scientific sign-off, and Phase 5 on
contract-owner sign-off. Nothing in this directory authorises activating
candidate arithmetic, changing q6, moving the methodology freeze, or shipping
"Your Food System Score™" on a canonical surface.
