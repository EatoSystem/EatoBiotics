01 · The Brand at a Glance

One idea. One framework.

EatoBiotics is built around a single mission: build the food system inside you, and help build the food system around you. Personal health and systemic change aren't separate goals — every plate feeds both.

The Idea

"Build the food system inside you… and help build the food system around you."

EatoBiotics is the inside layer of EatoSystem — a family of brands (EatoSports, EatoFund, EatoAI) using food as the operating system for human and planetary health.

The Framework

The 3 Biotics. Every piece of content, every color, and every feature maps back to:

  • Prebiotics — feed (fibers and compounds that nourish your gut bacteria)
  • Probiotics — add (live cultures from fermented foods)
  • Postbiotics — produce (the byproducts your microbiome creates)

Five stops. One gradient.

The brand palette is a 5-stop gradient extracted from the icon's biotic capsules. It is almost always deployed as a 135° gradient — solid brand color is the exception.

Gradient uses

  • Primary CTAs · 135° brand gradient with green-glow shadow
  • Top accent bars on cards (h-1.5)
  • Section dividers · 4px full-width 90° gradient
  • Headline clauses · clip-path text on a single phrase
  • Score rings · stroke gradient on the progress arc

Pair gradients

  • Prebiotic — Lime → Green
  • Probiotic — Green → Teal
  • Postbiotic — Yellow → Orange

Don't

  • Don't add new gradient stops or hues
  • Don't use solid orange/yellow as a primary action color
  • Don't apply the gradient to body text

04 · Content Fundamentals

Voice & Tone.

A practical, optimistic, second-person guide — speaks like a thoughtful coach, not a doctor, not a marketer.

Tone

  • Warm & optimistic. Aspirational but grounded — invites, never lectures.
  • Practical over academic. Science is earned but always tied to a next step.
  • Calm authority. Short, declarative sentences. No hype, no exclamation marks.
  • Systems-minded. Reaches for the word system constantly.

Casing

  • Sentence case for buttons, most headings, body.
  • Title Case on big brand-weight section titles.
  • UPPERCASE + .15em tracking for eyebrows.

Punctuation

  • Em-dashes, often.
  • Oxford comma, consistently.
  • Curly quotes in body. No exclamation marks. Ever.

Signature copy patterns

  • "The [thing] inside [possessive]" — the hero construction.
  • Triads. Feed. Add. Produce. / Assess. Score. Log. Improve.
  • Numbered steps — always zero-padded: 01, 02, 03.

Examples to copy from

An advanced system to understand your microbiome that improves how you feel every day — digestion, immunity, energy, mood, and recovery.
Hero subcopy — em-dash reveal, five nouns.
Build this plate once. Eat it five days. Change it next week.
The Plate caption — triad, imperative.
Build the food system inside you… and help build the food system around you.
The Manifesto — mirrored clauses, ellipsis pause.

07 · Rules of the road

Do & don't.

Do

  • Use the brand gradient on CTAs, score rings, and exactly one phrase per headline.
  • Lead with sentence case. Reserve title case for brand-weight statements.
  • Pair every claim with a next step.
  • Use the section divider (4px gradient) between major homepage sections.
  • Animate score counters 0 → N over ~1s.
  • Set Lora at -0.02em letter-spacing for big display.
  • Use real food photography on marketing surfaces.
  • Always use "you" as the subject.

Don't

  • Don't use exclamation marks in marketing copy. Ever.
  • Don't apply the gradient to body text or icons.
  • Don't add new colors outside the 5-stop palette.
  • Don't use emoji on marketing surfaces or in headings.
  • Don't substitute fonts (no Inter, no Roboto, no Sora, no Fraunces).
  • Don't use parallax, sticky scrollytelling, or bouncy spring physics.
  • Don't use "we" except in Jason's voice or company-level statements.
  • Don't lecture. Don't moralize. Don't use the word "guilt-free."
